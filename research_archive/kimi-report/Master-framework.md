The distinction must be mechanical, not semantic. "Absorbs kinetic energy vs. is unbreakable" (24.6k views). "Revenue vs income" (283 views). If the difference is a dictionary nuance, reject it.
The misconception must already exist. Every hit corrected a belief people actually hold ("Carnage is just a red Venom"). If you can't phrase the wrong belief in 10 words, the topic has no second hook.
Length ceiling: 31 seconds / ~140 words. Topics requiring 3+ sub-pairs or 40+ seconds of context fail (43–65% APV band).
Timeliness is a multiplier, not a foundation. JJK Terms (24.5k) rode an active anime season; Superman/Shazam (25k) rode DC's 2026 slate. Evergreen confusion + current hype is the ideal intersection.
Section 2 — Mandatory research protocol (do this first, every run)
Before scoring any topic, perform fresh research using whatever tools are available in that session. Do not rely on this document's examples being current — hype decays.

Franchise release calendar sweep. Search for: upcoming/recent MCU films & Disney+ series, DCU films & HBO series, major anime seasons (JJK, One Piece, Solo Leveling, Chainsaw Man, Demon Slayer, Boruto, Naruto spinoffs), major game releases (Marvel's Wolverine, etc.), top streaming genre shows (The Boys, Invincible, House of the Dragon, The Witcher). Record everything released within the last 60 days or releasing within the next 90 days.
Search-demand check. For each candidate pair: YouTube autocomplete on "X vs Y" and "difference between X and Y"; Google autocomplete; Google Trends comparison (X vs Y, 90-day window) where accessible. Record directional findings (rising/flat/spiking), never invented numbers.
Confusion check. Verify a real misconception exists: Reddit threads, Quora questions, comment-section arguments, "people also ask" boxes. One linkable confusion source per candidate.
Saturation check. Search YouTube for the exact pair. If the first page has 3+ million-view Shorts/videos making the identical distinction, mark competition high — proceed only with a differentiated angle (newer content, different axis of contrast).
State tool limitations explicitly. If Trends/autocomplete/Reddit are unreachable in the session, say so and substitute the strongest available proxy. Never fabricate search volumes or trends data.
Section 3 — Scoring model (0–100)
Score each candidate on 8 dimensions, 0–10 each, then apply weights:

Factor	Weight	What a 10 looks like
Current Search Interest	20%	Both entities actively searched; autocomplete surfaces the pair
Trend Momentum	15%	Entity appears in a release from the last 60 / next 90 days
Audience Confusion	15%	Documented, ongoing debate/misconception (Reddit/PAA/comment wars)
Curiosity Gap	15%	The true answer surprises even fans ("Red Hulk gets weaker when angrier")
Viral Potential	15%	Argument-bait: fans will fight in comments and tag friends
Visual/Conceptual Contrast	10%	Two instantly distinguishable visuals; difference drawable in one frame
Current Hype/Relevance	5%	Active news cycle this week
Competition Opportunity	5%	High interest + thin/shorts-poor competition
Reject gates (auto-reject regardless of score): obscure entity; dictionary-only distinction; needs >140 words to explain; factually contested answer; no visual contrast; saturated with no new angle.
